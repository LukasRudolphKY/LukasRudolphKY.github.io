    METHOD / REFERENCE

    What the metrics mean

    OEE is measured against planned production

    Planned = scheduled shift − excluded breaks. Run time = planned − downtime. Availability = run/planned. Performance = ideal seconds for all output/run seconds. Quality = ideal seconds for good first-pass output/ideal seconds for all output. OEE = good ideal seconds/planned seconds.

    Takt is driven by demand, not machinery

    Takt = planned production seconds ÷ required good units. Compare it to actual average good-output interval cautiously: multi-product mixes require equivalent units or separate analysis. Meeting demand does not necessarily require maximizing OEE.

    What dollars actually represent

    Downtime exposure = downtime hours × entered hourly economic impact. Modeled opportunity = targeted recovered hours × that same rate. Neither value proves recoverable profit, expense reduction, or financial return.

    Improvement before higher output

    If output meets demand, recovered capacity can support earlier cleanup, preventive inspections, setup preparation, 5S, employee training, verification and maintenance planning—in accordance with local procedures.

    Methodology: OEE.com OEE calculation · Lean Enterprise Institute takt time. Industry benchmarks are deliberately not imposed.
