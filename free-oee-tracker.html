How to calculate OEE

What is OEE?

Overall Equipment Effectiveness compares productive manufacturing time to planned production time. OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality.

Availability = operating time ÷ planned production time. Operating time = planned time − unplanned downtime.

How are multiple components handled?

When part cycle times differ, Performance uses the sum of ideal time for every unit produced divided by actual operating time. Quality uses good ideal production time divided by total ideal production time, instead of simply averaging unlike components.

This keeps the combined OEE equivalent to ideal seconds for good parts ÷ planned production seconds.

How does recorded cycle time help?

Record the measured cycle time to compare it with your process's proven ideal capability. A slow observed cycle may identify speed loss. It is a diagnostic input; do not substitute it for verified actual part counts when calculating standard OEE.

Why might my OEE exceed 100%?

A value above 100% indicates inconsistent entries or an ideal cycle time slower than your actual process capability. Review planned time, downtime, counts, and ideal cycle standards. The tracker flags this instead of silently altering your values.
