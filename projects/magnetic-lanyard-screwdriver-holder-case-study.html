A Compact Magnetic Screwdriver Bit Holder

A small component with several practical requirements: keep a screwdriver bit accessible, secure it with a magnet, and attach the holder to the device where it is used.

Problem and Design Goal

A small screwdriver bit is useful only when it can be found and accessed when needed. I wanted a compact holder that could remain attached to the device it serves, help prevent dropped or misplaced bits, and be easy to handle.

The primary constraint was size. The holder needed a small footprint while still accommodating bit retention, an attachment feature, and a usable grip.

Design Constraints and Decisions

Small Footprint

Kept the overall form compact instead of adding a bulky external clip or separate carrying case.

Embedded Magnet

Integrated a magnet into the printed body to retain the screwdriver bit in its socket.

Custom Threaded Connection

Designed matching threads for the two-piece construction. The thread fit is one of the functional features I verified in the printed assembly.

Attachment and Grip

Included a slotted loop for attachment to the intended device and surface texture to make the holder easier to handle.

Finished Design and Function

The result is a two-piece 3D-printed holder with a threaded body, a retained screwdriver bit, an integrated magnet, and a tethering point. The combination is intended to reduce the risk of leaving a loose bit on the shop floor and to keep it close to its point of use.

My original custom threads function in the printed parts, and the magnet holds the bit in position. I have not published dimensional tolerances, measured retention force, or durability-cycle test data for this design.

What This Project Demonstrates

This is not a complex assembly, and it doesn't need to be. The design work was in combining limited space, mechanical fit, magnetic retention, attachment, and user grip into a single practical object.

Relevant skills include CAD part modeling, threaded component design, FDM print planning, and functional detail integration.
