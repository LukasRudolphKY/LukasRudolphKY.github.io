Real Mechanical Design & 3D Printing Projects

Laser Engraver Ventilation Adapter

Problem: Route laser engraver exhaust through a window using a printable, fitted adapter.

Design approach: A first prototype informed a more open honeycomb geometry and a final PETG-CF print. Airflow improvements are a design intention, not instrument-tested results.

Read the design case study →

Magnetic Lanyard Screwdriver Holder

Problem: Keep a small screwdriver bit secured and attached to the device where it's used.

Design approach: Combine compact geometry, a working custom threaded body, embedded magnetic retention, textured grip, and an attachment loop.
