Professional Profile

I'm an experienced industrial technical professional with a diverse background in industrial maintenance, mechanical systems, automation, troubleshooting, and specialized technical operations. I currently work as a Non-Destructive Assay (NDA) Technician supporting environmental cleanup operations at the U.S. Department of Energy's Paducah site.

My professional interests extend beyond equipment operation and maintenance into computer-aided design, mechanical development, and engineering technology. I use SolidWorks, AutoCAD, and Autodesk Inventor to develop mechanical models, assemblies, and technical drawings, combining practical industrial knowledge with modern design tools.

Outside my professional responsibilities, I enjoy designing and building functional automation systems. One example is an automated poultry access system that I developed using SolidWorks, 3D printing, and Arduino microcontrollers. The project incorporated a photoresistor for light-based door operation, a linear actuator, relays, custom-designed components, and a selector switch for automatic or manual control. I designed, fabricated, programmed, wired, and assembled the system, demonstrating the integration of mechanical design, electronics, and embedded controls.

My approach to technical challenges emphasizes practical problem-solving, continuous learning, and developing solutions that are both functional and reliable. I'm particularly interested in opportunities that combine hands-on industrial experience with CAD design, automation, prototyping, and technical development.

Featured Project · SolidWorks + 3D Printing

Laser Engraver Ventilation Adapter

I designed and 3D printed a custom adapter to route a laser engraver's exhaust hose through a window. Starting with a white prototype featuring small perforations, I refined the geometry in SolidWorks to create a more open honeycomb design while using less printing material. The final adapter was produced in black PETG-CF.

Design evolution: White prototype → airflow-focused redesign → final PETG-CF print.

Skills: SolidWorks CAD, iterative prototyping, FDM 3D printing, material selection, functional design.

Featured Project · Functional Design + 3D Printing

Magnetic Lanyard Screwdriver Holder

I designed and 3D printed a compact, two-piece holder for a small flat-blade screwdriver bit. A magnet embedded in the base retains the bit in its socket, while the threaded top provides a slotted attachment for a lanyard. The design keeps a frequently needed screwdriver close at hand for quick access.

Design features: Magnetic bit retention, threaded two-piece construction, and lanyard attachment.
